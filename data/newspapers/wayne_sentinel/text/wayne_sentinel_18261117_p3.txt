White H ills.— Nearly one hundred and fifty men, and about half that number of oxen are now employed in repairing the turnpike road pacing through the Notch of the White Hills ; it is expected that it will be made passable for sleighs the com­ ing winter. The turnpike road is about twenty miles in length, on which were" twenty three bridges, twenty one of which were swept away by the freshet of August 28.— lb.

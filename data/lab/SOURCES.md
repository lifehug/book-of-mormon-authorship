# data/lab/

The lab's registry (`docs/lab/README.md`).

| File | What | Rebuild |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `registry.parquet` | one row for each catalogued work: source, kind, has text, corrected year, words, genre, duplicate keeper, OCR exclusion, author kind, swept author and life status, style-model split, language-model training split, in the passage index, sealed. Ids and flags only, no text. | `python -m lab.registry build` (seconds; reads `data/catalog/`, `data/ocr_quality/volumes.parquet`, `data/timelocked_lm/training_set.parquet`, `data/sweep/people.parquet`, `data/style_embed/authors.parquet`, and the shard tables of `data/corpus/semantic/index/`) |
| `registry.json` | column meanings, value counts, and what each rule set (`lab.views` in the config) admits | the same command |
| `sources.json` | the record of every outside source fetched for the new tests (BOM-99, BOM-102 to BOM-108): exact address, terms, where it sits on disk, size, SHA-256, date, and whether the fetch succeeded. Names and numbers only. The files themselves are in `data/corpus/lab/sources/` (open material; local disk and backup) and `data/private/lab/sources/` (copyrighted; private repository). | `python -m lab.sources fetch` (about an hour; resumes), then `python -m lab.sources marc` for the translation labels. Why each source: `docs/research/2026-11-18-sources-for-new-tests.md` |
| `reruns/mosiah_priority/result.json` | BOM-120: counts of "therefore" and "wherefore" in the 1830 text by block, book and narrator; the one-break fits; the same fit on 600 sampled library works (work ids and statistics). No text. | `python -m lab.wherefore run`; test card in the private repository, `lab/owner/2026-11-20-therefore-wherefore/card.md` |
| `reruns/isaiah_variants/townsend_items.json` | BOM-122: the 11 Isaiah passages Townsend (2025) ties to Adam Clarke's commentary, each with its verse, Townsend's pages, the claimed link and Jackson's objection in a few words. Rebuilt from Jackson's open reply (`data/private/lab/sources/papers/jackson2025_reply_to_townsend.pdf`; address in `sources.json`), not from Townsend's paywalled article; not yet checked against it. | keyed by hand from Jackson's reply, 2026-11-20 |
| `reruns/archaic_vocabulary/items_skousen2018_article.json` | BOM-118: 74 items from Skousen's open 2018 summary article (48 archaic word uses and phrases; 12 words once dated after 1750 and now dated earlier; 6 found only in later English; 5 never found in English; 3 rare King James uses), each with the meaning he assigns and the verse he cites. A selection from the 64 archaic items of his book; parsed by rule, not yet hand-checked. | parsed 2026-11-20 from `data/private/lab/sources/papers/skousen2018_language_of_the_original_text.pdf` (saved by hand by the owner; address in `sources.json`) |
| `sources_by_hand.json` | the same record for files the owner saved by hand in a browser because the site refuses programs (13 papers so far): address, terms, size, SHA-256. Merged into `sources.json` whenever the fetch command runs. | saved by hand; the addresses are in the file |
| `reruns/claimed_sources/result.json` | BOM-126: ranks of the claimed sources (Warren, Ramsay, Bunyan, Foxe, Milton, the Westminster Confession, the Apocrypha) among all library volumes on shared rare phrases and on meaning, and their share of the nearest passages to Abinadi and the war chapters; the known-answer checks. Ids, ranks and counts, no text. | `python -m lab.claimed_sources run`; card in the private repository, `lab/owner/2026-11-20-claimed-sources/card.md` |
| `setting/numbers/result.json` | BOM-114: number counts and profiles for the comparison texts, the base-twenty measure for each text, the known-answer checks, and the Alma 11 fit with its random baselines (pilot; the catalogue of weight systems is from memory and unsourced). Counts, no text. | `python -m lab.numbers run`; card in the private repository, `lab/owner/2026-11-20-numbers/card.md` |
| `names/result.json` | BOM-108: the Book of Mormon's non-biblical names (163), the name lists' sizes and spelling conventions, the known-answer and spelling checks, shares closest to each list with ranges, recombination counts. Names and numbers only. | `python -m lab.names run`; card in the private repository, `lab/owner/2026-11-20-names/card.md` |
| `setting/placenames/result.json` | BOM-110: the book's 57 non-biblical place names, match counts around Palmyra and around every comparison region at two thresholds and two radii, control name sets, period gazetteers by region, and Holley's 29 pairs scored. Names and numbers. | `python -m lab.placenames run`; card in the private repository, `lab/owner/2026-11-20-place-names/card.md` |
| `reruns/archaic_vocabulary/pilot.json` | BOM-118 pilot: raw hit counts by decade for ten items in 2,700 sampled works, and the known-answer check of the model judge (simulated judging), which missed its bar (7 of 10; bar 8), so no library passage was judged. Ids and counts, no text. | `python -m lab.archaic ...` (run guide); card in the private repository |
| `reruns/new_testament_wording/result.json` | BOM-124: New Testament-only phrase rates by section of the Book of Mormon and for every comparison text and sample, the known-answer checks, the step test, the share by New Testament book. Ids, counts and rates. | `python -m lab.ntwording run`; card in the private repository, `lab/owner/2026-11-20-new-testament-wording/card.md` |
| `reruns/emode_syntax/result.json` | BOM-119: the "did + verb" rate and nine other grammar features for the Book of Mormon, the King James Bible, the controls and the whole library by half-century; the works at or above the Book of Mormon's rate (ids, years, rates); the known-answer checks. No text. | `python -m lab.emode lexicon`, `check`, `pass`, `run`; card, hand check and reprint judgments in the private repository, `lab/owner/2026-11-20-emode-syntax/` |
| `reruns/manuscript_errors/result.json` | BOM-127: counts of corrections by type and scribe in the original manuscript, the printer's manuscript, the Book of Moses manuscripts and Letterbook 1; the known-answer checks; first spellings of names. | `two_voices/set.json` | BOM-99: the known-answer set for the scripture voice: six writers with both voices above 5,000 words (which files or ranges are which voice, word counts), and fifteen left out with the reason. No text. | `python -m lab.twovoices set` |
| `two_voices/result.json` | BOM-105: each writer's gap between voices, cross-voice attribution, the known-answer check, the variants. Labels and numbers. | `python -m lab.twovoices run`; card in the private repository, `lab/owner/2026-11-20-two-voices/card.md` |

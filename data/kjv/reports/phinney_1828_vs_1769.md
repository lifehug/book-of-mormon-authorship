# 1828 Phinney (Cooperstown) printing vs the 1769 digital text

Raw OCR of the Phinney scan (Internet Archive `holybiblecontain00cann`, with Apocrypha) against the eBible verses. A word counts as reached when it sits inside a five-word sequence that occurs anywhere in the OCR; a verse counts as found when at least half its words are reached. Order is ignored because the scan's two columns and marginal references scramble sequence. OCR errors lower the figure, so it is a floor on agreement, not a count of real variants.

- Verses found: 24,649 of 36,822 (66.9%)
- Words reached: 602,972 of 934,216 (64.5%)

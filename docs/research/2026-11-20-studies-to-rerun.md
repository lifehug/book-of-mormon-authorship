# Earlier studies of the Book of Mormon text that the library could rerun

2026-11-20. The owner asked which significant research on the Book of Mormon could now be
replicated, extended or shown wrong with the library and models in one place. An AI research agent
surveyed the literature from believing, critical and neutral authors. Not checked by a person.
Status: **O** = the agent opened the page; **F** = found in search results, not opened; **U** =
from the agent's memory, unchecked. Already rerun in the study and not listed here: Larsen,
Rencher and Layton 1980; Holmes 1992; Jockers, Witten and Criddle 2008; Schaalje et al. 2011;
Johnson and Johnson 2013; *View of the Hebrews*, *The Late War*, *The First Book of Napoleon*;
Grunder's parallels. Issues: BOM-118 to BOM-127.

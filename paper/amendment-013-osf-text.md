# Amendment 13: the rare-phrase source list

*Filed 2026-11-01. Evidence: `docs/rare-plan.md`.*

---

**Amendment 13, filed 2026-11-01, before the known-answer scans for rare phrases and before the
Book of Mormon's source list is computed. Partly post hoc:** the Book of Mormon's rare 4-grams
were already counted against every period volume in the registered replication of Johnson and
Johnson (2013), and its ranking under the registered rule was seen there. The additions below
were decided after that result. The registered score (5.5), threshold, null (Amendment 7) and
document-frequency count by text (Amendment 8) do not change, and significance is decided by
them alone.

**Dates.** The population is every eligible volume dated 1780-1830 after the date audit of
Amendment 12. Volumes the audit moved out of the period are not in the population, null or
document frequencies; they are scored with the same document frequencies and listed separately
with both dates, because some of them may truly be early.

**Additions reported beside the registered result (post hoc).** Each source's percentile within
its own primary genre label. 95% intervals for each listed source's score and p-value, by
resampling the Book of Mormon's masked text in 1,000-word blocks (1,000 resamples) with document
frequencies held fixed. *The Late War* and *The First Book of Napoleon* ranked within the curated
pseudo-biblical list of Amendment 6 (18 works), so that a style shared with imitation scripture is
compared like with like. The named sources (*The Late War*, *View of the Hebrews*, *The First Book
of Napoleon*, Spalding's *Manuscript Story*, and the King James Gospel of Luke as a source against
the masked text) are reported whatever they score.

**Known answers (not yet run).** For Amendment 9, each text's score is the strongest source's
rare shared 4-grams per 10,000 words in each of the three matched-window draws of Amendment 10,
with document frequency counted within the draw by text, averaged over the draws. If the
separation rule fails, the rare-phrase list is reported as uninformative on the translation
question.

# Amendment 40: the sweep's plausibility gate, read precisely (BOM-47)

*Filed 2026-11-07. Evidence: `docs/plausibility-gate.md`, `data/sweep/gate_sample.csv`.*

---

**Amendment 40, filed 2026-11-07, before any sweep lead is seen (the sealed text is scored but not
unblinded).** Section 3's rule for adding candidates requires "documented historical plausibility
(period, place, or access)". Read literally, any one criterion suffices, and period alone passes
94 of 100 randomly drawn swept authors, so the gate would not filter. It is fixed as: **period**
(alive and writing in English in 1800-mid 1829, or published in time to circulate then) **and**
(**place**: documented presence in, or local distribution in, western/central New York or
northeastern Pennsylvania before mid-1829, **or access**: documented availability near
Palmyra/Manchester/Harmony/Fayette by BOM-33's standards). Applied blind to scores to the 100
gate-sample authors (seed `bom47-gate`): 10 pass (95% interval 5.5-17.4%), so of the 0.035 false
leads expected per query, about 0.0035 would pass by chance. Any sweep lead is researched with the
same search procedure, no deeper, so the comparison holds. The literal reading (94%), a lenient
reading (any American printing before mid-1829 counts, 35%) and the rate without two borderline
calls (8%) are reported beside it.
